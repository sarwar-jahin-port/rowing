Why Oar & Blade

We are not a general creative studio that stumbled into rowing. Every decision we make — content angle, caption tone, visual style — is informed by genuine rowing knowledge and community insight.

Deep Rowing Context

We understand the difference between a sculling club and a sweep program, between a novice regatta and a head race. That knowledge changes what content gets made and how it lands.

Club-Fit Content

Every post is shaped around your specific culture, colors, and community — not a template reshuffled from last month's client. Supporters can feel it is genuinely from your club.

Handover-Proof System

When your volunteer social media lead leaves, the content keeps going. We build a documented rhythm so no single person holds the keys to your online presence.

Goal-Aligned Output

Whether you want to recruit junior athletes, attract sponsors, or grow alumni engagement — we prioritize content that moves the needle on your specific goals, not vanity metrics.

Less Volunteer Burden

We take the planning, production, and scheduling off your committee's plate. You focus on the water. We handle what goes online — with as much or as little involvement as you prefer.

Clear Monthly Scope
